# On-call and Incident Response

## Rotation
Engineers on the platform team join a weekly on-call rotation, Monday 10:00 to the next Monday
10:00. Each engineer is on call at most once every 6 weeks.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive a stipend of 300 EUR per week on call, plus time off in lieu for any
incident work between 22:00 and 07:00.

## Severity levels
- SEV1: customer robots are down or unsafe. Acknowledge within 5 minutes; all hands.
- SEV2: major feature degraded for many customers. Acknowledge within 15 minutes.
- SEV3: minor issue with a workaround. Handle within the next working day.

## Post-incident reviews
Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident gets a blameless post-incident review within 5 working days.
