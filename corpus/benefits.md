# Benefits

## Health
The company pays private health insurance for employees and their dependants. Dental cover is
included; optical cover is reimbursed up to 200 EUR every two years.

## Pension
Lumen Robotics matches pension contributions up to 6% of base salary.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,500 EUR per year for courses, conferences and books, plus
5 learning days. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Wellbeing
Employees get a gym or wellbeing allowance of 40 EUR per month, and free access to a counselling
service.
