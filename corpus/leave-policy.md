# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days may be
carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Requesting leave
Requests are made in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.
Your line manager approves or declines within 5 working days.

## Sick leave
Employees may self-certify sickness for up to 3 consecutive days. From the 4th day a doctor's note
is required. Sick leave is paid at full salary for up to 20 days per year.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents receive 12 weeks of
fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.
