# Business Travel

## Booking
All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the TravelDesk portal. Book at least 14 days in
advance where possible.

## Class of travel
Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
Trains are booked in standard class, except journeys over 4 hours, which may be first class.

## Hotels
The nightly hotel limit is 180 EUR in most cities and 250 EUR in London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich.

## Travel insurance
Every business trip is covered by the company travel insurance policy. Download the insurance
certificate from the TravelDesk portal before you travel.
