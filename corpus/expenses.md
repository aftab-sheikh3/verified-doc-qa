# Expenses Policy

## What can be claimed
Reasonable costs incurred on company business: travel, accommodation, client meals and work
equipment that was approved in advance by your manager.

## Limits
- Client meals: up to 60 EUR per person, including drinks.
- Meals while travelling alone: up to 45 EUR per day.
- Home-office equipment: a one-off allowance of 500 EUR in your first year.

## Submitting claims
Claims are submitted in the Expensify app within 30 days of the expense, with a photo of the
receipt. Claims above 1,000 EUR need approval from the department head. Approved claims are paid
with the next monthly salary.
