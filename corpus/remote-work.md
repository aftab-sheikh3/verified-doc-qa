# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Office days
Teams work in a hybrid model: at least 2 days per week in the office, chosen by each team.
Hardware and robotics lab staff work on site.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per year, with manager approval and a
check by HR for tax and visa rules. Working from countries under sanctions is never allowed.

## Equipment
Remote workers get a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Additional equipment is covered by the
home-office allowance described in the expenses policy.
