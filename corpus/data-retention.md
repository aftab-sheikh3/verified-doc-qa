# Data Retention

## Customer telemetry
Raw robot telemetry is kept for 90 days, then aggregated. Aggregated, anonymised telemetry is kept
for 5 years.

## Email and chat
Work email is retained for 7 years. Chat messages in public channels are retained for 2 years;
direct messages for 1 year.

## Employee records
HR records are kept for 6 years after an employee leaves. Recruitment data of unsuccessful
candidates is deleted after 12 months unless the candidate agrees to a longer period.

## Deletion requests
Customer data deletion requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed in writing.
