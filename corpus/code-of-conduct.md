# Code of Conduct

## Gifts
Employees may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to 50 EUR. Anything above that must be
declared to the compliance team within 7 days.

## Conflicts of interest
Side jobs and investments in competitors, suppliers or customers must be declared in the HR
portal before they start.

## Speaking up
Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation
against anyone who speaks up in good faith is a disciplinary offence.
